One-line intent: AIR Money gives a signed-in user a smart account they didn't ask for and don't manage, and lets the partner app move value through it without ever touching keys or funds.

## Outline for Cham to edit

- **Sign in.** The account belongs to the signed-in AIR Account. No separate wallet connect. See [User login](/get-started/authentication/login).
- **Smart account.** First transaction deploys a chain-specific smart account on the user's behalf. Deployment gas is sponsored. See [Smart accounts](/products/money/smart-account).
- **Hold / send / swap.** Standard EIP-1193 provider for signing and sending, plus hosted screens for send/receive/swap/history when the partner doesn't want to build them. See [Wallet functions](/products/money/provider-functions) and [Built-in UI](/products/money/builtin-ui).
- **No seed phrase.** The user never sees or stores a mnemonic. Recovery runs through the AIR Account.
- **Sponsored gas.** The partner configures a paymaster policy (allowlists, caps, per-user limits) and users pay no gas inside those rules. See [Paymaster](/products/money/paymaster).
- **What the partner does not custody.** Partner backends do not hold private keys or user funds. The partner app signs through the user's smart account via the provider; AIR does not sign on the partner's behalf and does not take custody of balances.
